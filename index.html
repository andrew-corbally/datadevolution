Why data devolution matters

High quality, timely and linked data helps local government see across boundaries and turn evidence into better decisions.

This prototype explores the opportunities to improve access to central government data. These datasets can help to deliver and plan services, monitor impact, anticipate pressures and develop data driven solutions in every postcode.

A starting point, not a complete catalogue
Improving the visibility of data held across departments is one part of the solution.

What the prototype prioritises

1

Timely access

Uses regular in-year and operational data, not just delayed snapshots.

2

Linked outcomes

Connects education, work, earnings, benefits, health and services to understand long-term results.

3

Nationwide and local detail

Combines a nationwide view with the granularity needed for local delivery.

4

Better discovery

Makes departmental data easier to find and creates a route for ad-hoc data enquiries.

About this prototype

This prototype service is based on the principle of providing access to centrally held government data to Mayoral Strategic Authorities and Local Authorities.

It is a prototype designed for discovery, with seven priority themes.
