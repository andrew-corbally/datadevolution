Why data devolution matters

High-quality, timely and linked data helps regional and local government see across boundaries and turn evidence into better decisions.

The Greater London Authority's response sets out opportunities to improve access to central government data. The aim is practical: deliver and plan services, monitor impact, anticipate pressure and develop data-driven support for Londoners.

What the response prioritises

1

Timely access

Use regular in-year and operational data, not just delayed snapshots.

2

Linked outcomes

Connect education, work, earnings, benefits, health and services to understand long-term results.

3

London-wide and local detail

Combine a capital-wide view with the granularity needed for local delivery.

4

Better discovery

Make departmental holdings easier to find and create a route for ad-hoc data enquiries.

About this prototype

This service is based on the Greater London Authority's Response to Rewiring the State: Central government data to improve public service delivery, dated October 2026.

It is an interpretation designed for discovery, not a replacement for the original response. Detailed request cards cover the portions of the source used in this prototype; the seven-area overview preserves the full thematic structure set out in the executive summary.
